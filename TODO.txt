[ ] Consider channel-level style analysis
    Currently Stage 5/6 only compare per-video: top N competitor videos'
    captions for one query. A channel-level signal would instead look at
    a competing channel's pattern across many videos (recurring formats,
    titling conventions, what topics they return to) rather than just a
    single video's transcript.
    From 2026-09-01 comparison against tubegen.ai (a commercial faceless-
    channel video-generation SaaS, not a real competitor to this tool's
    purpose) -- its "replicate a creator's style" feature does channel-
    level pattern matching, which is sharper than this project's current
    per-video-only competitor comparison. Worth a future Stage 3/6 signal,
    not urgent.
